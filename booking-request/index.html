Request a booking

Thank you for thinking of us. This page explains how to start a booking. You do not book or pay through this website. We talk with you first, so we can understand your project before anything is agreed.

How it works

  1. Get in touch. Message us on WhatsApp or send an email with your project idea.
  2. We reply to discuss your project. We talk through your song, the film you have in mind and the next steps.
  3. If you want to go ahead, we send you payment instructions directly. Nothing is paid through this website.
  4. We confirm your booking in writing once the project is agreed and your payment has been received and checked.

What to include in your message
