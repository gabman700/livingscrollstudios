The process

From prompt to film

Generative AI is one part of a directed filmmaking pipeline. Every stage is shaped by creative decisions — not automation alone.

01

Concept & script

The story, message and emotional tone — turned into a script and shot list.

02

Visual development

Look, colour, world-building and character designs locked before production.

03

Character consistency

Reference sheets and multi-reference workflows keep every face the same, shot to shot.

04

Generative production

Image-to-video with the right model for each shot, run through controlled ComfyUI workflows.

05

Quality control

Every shot checked for identity drift, anatomy, artifacts and continuity — and regenerated if needed.

06

Edit, sound & delivery

Pacing, sound design, voice and music, then delivery in every format you need.
