How it works

Simple remote collaboration

01

Tell us about your project

Send the enquiry form below, or message us on WhatsApp or by email. We reply within 24–48 hours.

02

Brief & quote

We discuss your song, release date, visual style and budget, then send a clear written quote.

03

Concept approval

Once the deposit is paid, we share the concept, look and key characters for your approval before full production.

04

Production

We produce, edit and sync the video to your finished audio track.

05

Revisions

Your agreed revision rounds let you fine-tune the result.

06

Delivery

We deliver files ready for YouTube and social platforms, including vertical cut-downs if they're part of your package.

Time zones: We're on West Africa Time (GMT+1). That's the same as UK time in summer and one hour ahead in winter, so calls and replies fit within the UK working day.
