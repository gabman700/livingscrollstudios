How it works

  1. Choose a package below and request a rand quote on WhatsApp or by email.

  3. Receive your rand quote. We confirm the package, the total, the deposit and how to pay.
  4. Confirm payment of the deposit. The deposit is half of the total and secures your production slot.
  5. Production begins. The balance, the other half of the total, is due before final delivery.
