How it works

From package to production

  1. 1

    See the price in rand and choose a music video under 1 minute, under 2 minutes, or a full song.

  2. 2

    Click Book Now

    The Book Now button opens the secure Selar checkout for that package.

  3. 3

Deposit and balance

Deposit: 50%. The deposit secures your production slot. You pay it in rand when you book, through Selar. Balance: 50%. The balance is due before final delivery. We send you a private Selar payment link, in rand, when it is due.

Prices are in South African rand (ZAR). US dollar amounts are approximate equivalents based on the exchange rate on 3 November 2026; you are charged in rand.

A revision is one defined round of changes (adjustments or a scene modification). New scenes or concepts count as new work. Production takes 5 days, starting once your deposit and your song and brief have been received.

What to include in your brief

  • Your name, and the artist name
  • The song title and its approximate length
  • A link to the song or the audio file
  • The kind of film you have in mind, and any release date you are working towards

Paying your balance

The balance is due before final delivery. When it is due, we send you a private Selar link for your package's balance, in rand, on WhatsApp or by email.

Our quality commitment

If the video isn't up to standard

Our music videos are created using generative AI, and occasional visual inconsistencies or AI-generated artifacts may occur.

If your final video contains noticeable AI artifacts, visual inconsistencies, or other unintended elements that make it look obviously artificial, we will first make reasonable efforts to correct them.

If we are unable to resolve these issues to an acceptable standard, we will issue a refund.

Our goal is to deliver a cinematic video that meets the quality standard we promise.
